# Slide 02 — Problem Statement

---

## The Clinical Challenge

- Glaucoma is the **leading cause of irreversible blindness** worldwide
  - ~80 million people affected; projected 111.8 million by 2040
- The key challenge: **identifying fast progressors early** before irreversible vision loss

---

## Why Is This Hard?

- Current standard: **Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) linear regression** of Mean Deviation (MD) over time
- Requires **6–10 serial tests over several years** before a reliable slope can be established
- Slow progressors can be managed conservatively; fast progressors need **urgent treatment escalation**
- Missing a fast progressor = **irreversible functional disability**

---

## The Question

> Can deep learning on routine longitudinal visual field data predict glaucoma progression rate **faster and more accurately** than current clinical tools?
